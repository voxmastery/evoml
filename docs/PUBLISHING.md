## LinkedIn

I spent two weeks letting a model rewrite itself every 30 minutes, and
refusing to believe it until it beat a coin flip in public.

EvoML is a self-evolving prediction model with an honest scoreboard. It
predicts 15-minute direction on 30 live Solana tokens, owns its own genome
(features, architecture, threshold, mutation rate, a humility cap), runs a
tournament every half hour, and only crowns a challenger that wins by more
than one standard error. The neural network underneath is written from
scratch in NumPy and gradient-checked. It runs on a laptop with no AI API
key.

After 14.6 days and 572 live calls: 56.3 % accuracy (skill z = 3.0). The
random control on the same coins and windows: 50.5 %. Every pre-registered
gate passed. A statistical edge, not a money machine, and I say so in the
README.

Why it matters beyond crypto: fraud, risk and pricing models decay too. The
reusable part is the loop that keeps measuring and keeps evolving.

Built for the Razorpay AI Buildathon (Open Track).

Adam, pruning, gradient-checked). Children inherit their parent's weights.

## Show HN

Title: Show HN: EvoML, a self-evolving prediction model with a random control
and an audit trail

Body: EvoML predicts 15-minute price direction on live Solana tokens and
rewrites its own genome every 30 minutes (feature chromosome, architecture,
threshold, mutation rate, humility cap; genetic programming invents new
features). Succession is significance-gated so noise cannot dethrone skill.
The neural net is from-scratch NumPy with hand-derived backprop and
finite-difference gradient checks; children inherit parent weights. What I
care most about is the measurement: every call is logged before the outcome
exists, resolved against the real price, and compared to a random control on
identical data with gates fixed before the run. After 14.6 days: 56.3 % vs
50.5 %, z = 2.3. Fee-inclusive capital is flat, which the README says up
front. Code and live site in the links; happy to discuss the failures (the
Claude arm I stopped, the confidence inversion, the lineage lost to noise).
